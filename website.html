Our journey started in our family kitchen, where recipes were passed down through generations. Every batch of Karapodi is prepared just the way our mother and grandmother made it—slow-roasted lentils, sun-dried chilies, fresh garlic, and aromatic spices.

We never compromise on quality. We source raw spices directly from trusted local farmers, roast them to perfection, and grind them in small quantities to ensure every jar brings back the comfort of home-cooked meals.
